// FREQUENTLY ASKED
What counts as one "agent run"?
Each time you submit a task to NEXUS, that's one run. A run can involve multiple tool calls internally — you're only counted once per task.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your dashboard at any time. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.
Is my data private?
Yes. Your tasks and agent outputs are stored only on your server instance. This is your own deployment.
Do I need a credit card for the free plan?
No. The free plan requires no payment information at all. Sign up with just your email.
